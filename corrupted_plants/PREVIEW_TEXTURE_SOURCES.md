# Preview-only dependencies

The plant sprites are new assets. The standalone viewer embeds existing ground textures only to demonstrate their appearance:

- dirt and coarse dirt: original Minecraft 1.21.1 textures, extracted asset mirror https://github.com/InventivetalentDev/minecraft-assets/tree/1.21.1/assets/minecraft/textures/block . Minecraft ground artwork belongs to Mojang/Microsoft.
- corrupted soil: the existing Fall of Herovgadia `corrupted_soil_top.png` in this repository; unchanged.
- Three.js r129 and OrbitControls: MIT; see THIRD_PARTY_LICENSE.txt.

No new ground blocks or changes to those textures are included in the integration resources.
